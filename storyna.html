The loop is the engine.

02 — Prototype
A working slice in days, not a deck. Real model, real data, real failure modes on the table by Friday.

        
03 — Ship
Deploy to people who'll be annoyed if it's wrong. That's the only test that counts for anything.

Read the traces, keep what survived contact, and feed everything back into the next lap around.

SEQ 08 // QUERIES

      
Frequently asked.

      
The things people ask before the first call. Edit these to match how you actually work.

    

    

      
> what kind of products does the lab build?
Placeholder answer. Name the category of problems you take on — and the ones you turn down. A sharp "not this" reads as more confident than a broad "anything."

      
> who owns the IP?
Placeholder answer. State the default plainly and where it can flex. This is the question that quietly kills deals when the answer is vague.

      
> how do you handle our data?
Placeholder answer. Retention, training usage, hosting, certifications. Be specific — hedging here reads as evasion.

      
> how fast is a first working version?
Placeholder answer. Give a real range, not a best case, and say what moves a project between the fast end and the slow end.

      
> what happens when a product doesn't work out?
Placeholder answer. Being candid about the units that got killed is a credibility signal. Most labs pretend it never happens.
